# ![The Graveyard](<../Images/The Graveyard.png>)

Please note: This route is only applicable to the Demo's Release Patch, so you may need to downpatch the game. Also, all the footage you see in this guide is from [3:05 WR made by me](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9A1mfmiEsS8).

## Beginning

The level starts off with a short 14-second cutscene, after that, you want to exit the ship as fast as possible. To do this, I go to the left corner close to a grunt and wait until SIX starts saying "Let's hope...". After that I jump + crouch to get in between the opening doors. Then you want to jump up the broken cars on the left side and jump down to the destructible door.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a8da4660-48b2-4278-a77b-3c607d09524d

## Middle Section and Nade Boost

As soon as you destroy the door, you want to jump onto the right car and jump up to skip the first mantle. To skip the second one, you want to jump into the corner of the platform and mantle the third wall. If you have a really good bunny pattern, you can bounce off the second platform up to the third one. Then you simply need to move as fast as possible until you meet the Judge.

Now the hardest part begins. You need to kill the Judge, climb up onto the door and pray for grunts to throw a nade. If you got the nade, you want to get as close to the nade as possible. As it explodes, jump and move forward on the roof.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/596cbe8e-62ae-42e4-8192-0abaf8f78eb9

## Barrel Boost and Level Ending

After you get onto the roof, you want to jump down, then grab the farthest barrel and trigger the grunts' spawn. Then you want to set up the barrel to make a barrel boost up to the bridge above you. I set it up the following way: go behind the static barrel, place the barrel in my hands so it is in front of me, look down and throw it. You can also get an additional boost from grunts, but it's pretty rare.

After you land on the bridge, jump forward until you see a large platform. Jump on it. Continue your BHop sequence and go in between two buildings. Then you want to BHop right into the end-of-level trigger and that's it. You've passed the first level!
