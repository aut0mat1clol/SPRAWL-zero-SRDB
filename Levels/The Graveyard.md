# ![The Graveyard](<../Images/The Graveyard.png>)

Please note: This route is only applicable for Demo's Release Patch, so you may need to downpatch the game. Also all footages you see in this guide are from [3:05 WR made by me](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9A1mfmiEsS8).

## Beginning

Level starts off with 14 seconds of a small cutscene, after that you want to exit the ship as fast as possible. To do this I go to the left corner close to a grunt and wait until SIX starts saying "Let's hope...". After that I jump+crouch to get inbetween the openning doors. Then you want to jump up the broken cars on the left side and jump down to destructable door.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a8da4660-48b2-4278-a77b-3c607d09524d

## Middle section and Nade boost

As soon as you destroy th door you want to jump on the right car and jump up to skip the first mantle. To skip the second one you want to jump into the corner of the platform and mantle the third wall. If you have really good bunny pattern you can bounce of the second platform up to the third one. Then you simply need to move as fast as possible until you meet the Judge.

Now the hardest part begins. You need to kill the Judge, climb up the door and pray for grunts to throw a nade. If you got the nade, you want to press the nade as close to you as possible. As it explodes, jump and move forward on the roof.

(TheGraveyardMiddle.mp4 video)

## Barrel boost and level ending

After you got onto the roof you want to jump down, then grab farthest barrel and trigger grunt's spawn. Then you want to setup the barrel to make a barrel boost up to the bridge above you. I setup it the next way: go behind the static barrel, place barrel in my hands so it is in front of me, look down and throw it. You can also get additional from grunts, but it's kinda rare.

After you landed on the bridge, jump forward until you see a large platform. Jump on it. Continue your Bhop sequence and go in between two buildings. Then you want to bhop right into the end level trigger and that's it. You've passed first level!
