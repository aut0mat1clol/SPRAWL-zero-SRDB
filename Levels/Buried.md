# ![Buried](../Images/Buried.png)

Unlike The Graveyard, fastest patch to play this level is current one. (No videos for now, I'll add them later).

## Beginning

As soon as you start, you want to jump, go backwards and turn around. After that you want to BHop as fast as possible to stim. You can jump back as soon as you pick up it, it saves some time. After you touched the first checkpoint, you need to get to the second one as fast as possible. Then the interesting part begins...

(BuriedBeginning.mp4 video)

## Double Barrel boost and Ending

So, you want to get on top of the two barrels and use your punch attack to explode them. You want to aim somewhere right from checkpoint you got. After the explotion, you want to do 3-4 Powered Crouch Hops and get ~3000 u/s. After your last jump you need to fly on top of the building on the opposite side of the "void". Your screen may become dark, don't worry. It's just an effect, you'll countinue jumping in right direction after that. 

(BuriedBoost.mp4 video)

After all of that you want to get to the level ending cutscene as soon as possible. You can try to use all of momentum you got to do a small loop and get in between two openning doors.

(BuriedEnding.mp4 video)

And this is the ending of the level and the demo! GL with your runs!
