# ![Buried](../Images/Buried.png)

Unlike The Graveyard, the fastest patch to play this level is the current one. (No videos for now — I'll add them later.)

## Beginning

As soon as you start, you want to jump, go backwards and turn around. After that, you want to BHop as fast as possible to the stim. You can jump back as soon as you pick it up — it saves some time. After you touch the first checkpoint, you need to get to the second one as fast as possible. Then the interesting part begins...

(BuriedBeginning.mp4 video)

## Double Barrel Boost and Ending

So, you want to get on top of the two barrels and use your punch attack to explode them. You want to aim somewhere to the right of the checkpoint you got. After the explosion, you want to do 3-4 Powered Crouch Hops and get ~3000 u/s. After your last jump, you need to fly to the top of the building on the opposite side of the "void". Your screen may become dark — don't worry. It's just an effect, you'll continue jumping in the right direction after that.

(BuriedBoost.mp4 video)

After all of that, you want to get to the level-ending cutscene as soon as possible. You can try to use all of the momentum you got to do a small loop and get in between two opening doors.

(BuriedEnding.mp4 video)

And this is the end of the level and the demo! GL with your runs!
