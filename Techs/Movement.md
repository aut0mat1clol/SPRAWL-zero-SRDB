## Basic Movement Mechanics

Game has sevral basic movement mechanics. Main ones are sprinting, jumping, sliding and, of cource, default movement. Player's speed is counted in units per second (u/s).

Default movement gives you 500 u/s:

![Default Speed](WebPs/DefaultSpeed.webp)

Sprint increases your speed up to 750 u/s and crouch reduses to 400 u/s:

![SprintSpeed](WebPs/SprintSpeed.webp) ![CrouchedSpeed](WebPs/CrouchedSpeed.webp)

Sliding adds 100 u/s to sprinting speed, bringing it to 850 u/s:

![SlideSpeed](WebPs/SlideSpeed.webp)

And jumping which gives player 750 u/s on vertical (Z) axis:

![JumpSpeed](WebPs/JumpSpeed.webp)

## Advanced Movement Techs

As soon as demo was release, many people discovered that game has bunnyhop. Bunnyhop (also known as BHop) is a technique when you continously press A and D and move your mouse in the direction of your movement. Unlike in the id Tech or Source engines, jumping on inclined surfaces here does not affect the player's speed.

![DefaultBhop](Avifs/DefaultBHop_30fps.avif)

I also should mention one simple technique which will be important later - Side Sliding. As the name says, you need to slide to the side, using W+A or W+D. This method of sliding adds ~300u/s to sprinting speed, bringing it up to 1050 u/s.

![SideSliding](Avifs/SideSliding.avif)

As you may know you can do slides and gain additional 100u/s. But, if we'll press crouch mid air you'll get additional 250-300 u/s. This is called Crouch Hopping.

![CrouchHopping](Avifs/CrouchHopping.avif)

And if we'll combine Crouch Hopping and Side Sliding techniques we would get even more speed. Instead of 250 u/s speed boost we'll get 400 u/s boost. This is called Powered Crouch Hopping. You can get up to 3300 u/s using only 5 Side Slides, while with default Crouch Hops give you around 2750 u/s.

![PowerCrouchHopping](Avifs/PowerCrouchHopping.avif)
